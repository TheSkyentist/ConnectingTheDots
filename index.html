About the Conference

Image placeholder

Connecting the Dots is a focused workshop dedicated to the emerging class of compact, high-redshift sources known as Little Red Dots (LRDs) — identified in large numbers by JWST across the early Universe.

The meeting brings together observers, theorists, and modellers to share recent results, debate interpretations, and chart a course for future multi-wavelength follow-up. Topics include the nature of LRD spectra and photometry, their connection to black-hole and galaxy co-evolution, dust geometry, and AGN demographics at cosmic dawn.

The workshop format prioritises discussion over presentations. Attendance is limited to encourage candid exchange.

Venue

Image placeholder

The conference takes place at Ringberg Castle, the Max Planck Society's retreat nestled in the Bavarian Alps above Tegernsee, Germany.

The castle's remote setting, self-contained accommodation, and intimate seminar rooms make it ideal for a focused research workshop. All participants stay on site, fostering informal discussion around the clock.

Ringberg is accessible by train to Tegernsee followed by a short taxi ride. Detailed travel instructions will be circulated to registered participants.
